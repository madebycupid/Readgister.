An archive of a school library system called Readgister (2025).

Readgister is a project developed by a group of second-year college students, primarily led by me, Charlie. This project serves as our final assignment for the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology program.

Readgister makes book tracking simple and smart! It helps students and librarians keep track of borrowed books quickly and efficiently.
